Privacy Policy

Trendscope · last updated 8 October 2026

The short version. Trendscope runs entirely on your own computer. It has no server, no user accounts and no analytics. Nothing it reads from your Google account is transmitted to the developer or to any third party, because there is nowhere for it to be transmitted to.

Who operates this software

Trendscope is a personal, open project maintained by an individual. It is distributed as source code that you run yourself. The developer operates no servers and receives no data from installations.

What Google data Trendscope accesses

If — and only if — you choose to connect a Google account, Trendscope requests these OAuth scopes:

  • openid and userinfo.email — to show which account is connected and to tell two connected accounts apart.
  • calendar.readonly — to read the calendars on that account.

The read-only calendar scope is requested deliberately. Trendscope displays your events; it cannot create, change or delete anything in your Google account, and no narrower scope exposes the event times it needs.

How that data is used

Calendar events are shown in Trendscope's own calendar, alongside your local tasks, reminders and reviews, so you can plan around real commitments. They may also trigger a local desktop notification before an event starts, if you have switched alerts on.

Where it is stored

  • Event titles, times, locations and descriptions are cached in a SQLite database file on your computer (data/trendscope.db).
  • OAuth access and refresh tokens are stored in that same local file.
  • Your OAuth client ID and secret live in a local .env file.

None of these files leave your machine. Trendscope makes no outbound connection other than directly to Google's own API endpoints and to the public news and job sources you configure.

Sharing

Google user data is never sold, never shared with third parties, never used for advertising, and never used to train any model. It is not transmitted to the developer. Trendscope has no telemetry and no crash reporting.

Retention and deletion

Cached events are replaced each time you sync and are deleted outright when you disconnect the account or switch a calendar off. Disconnecting an account in Trendscope also asks Google to revoke the token.

You can remove everything at any time by deleting data/trendscope.db, or revoke Trendscope's access independently at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Limited Use

Trendscope's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Security

Because the application is local, your Google tokens are protected by your operating system account and whatever disk encryption you use — the same protection as any other file on your computer. Anyone with access to your user account could read them, so treat data/trendscope.db and .env as you would any other credential file.

Children

Trendscope is not directed at children and collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of this page changes with it.

Questions about this policy: use the support email address shown on the Google consent screen when you connect an account.
