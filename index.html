Trendscope

A personal reading, planning and study tool that runs on your own computer.

Trendscope collects technology news, job postings and research into a local database, and turns them into a reader, a skills dashboard, a project board, a calendar and a study planner. It runs entirely on your own machine as a local web application at 127.0.0.1, with an optional tray app for Windows.

What it does

  • Reads articles, discussions, papers and podcasts in one place.
  • Tracks which skills job postings are actually asking for.
  • Plans work on a board, a timeline and a calendar.
  • Tracks job applications and interview preparation.
  • Schedules repeating reminders such as pay dates and birthdays.
  • Shows your Google Calendar alongside all of the above, read-only.

Google Calendar

Trendscope can sign in to Google to read your calendars, so that real commitments appear next to your own tasks and reminders. It never creates, edits or deletes anything in your Google account: it requests only the read-only calendar scope.

Everything it reads is stored in a database file on your own computer. There is no Trendscope server, no account to create, and nothing is uploaded anywhere. See the privacy policy for the detail.

Running it

Trendscope needs Python 3.10 or newer and nothing else — no installation step, no dependencies to download. Start it with python run.py for the web app, or python app.py on Windows for the version that lives in the notification area.
